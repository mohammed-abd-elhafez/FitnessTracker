Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What the app reads

With your permission, FitnessTracker reads steps, walking and running distance, active energy and workouts from Apple Health to show your daily progress.

Where your data stays

Health data and the workouts and goals you enter are stored on your device only. The app has no account system and no server, and it does not send your data anywhere.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share your data.
  • We do not use health data for advertising or tracking.
  • We do not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Your control

You can change or revoke access at any time in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices. Deleting the app removes the data it stored.

Medical disclaimer

FitnessTracker is for general fitness tracking and is not a medical device or a diagnostic tool.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, open an issue in the FitnessTracker GitHub repository.
